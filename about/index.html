About

I am a master’s student in mathematics at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin.

Academic background

I completed my bachelor’s degree in mathematics at Technische Universität Berlin in 2024. My bachelor’s thesis, Algebraic Curves and the Riemann–Roch Theorem, was supervised by Prof. Dr. Peter Bürgisser and Dr. Dominic Bunnett.

I am now studying mathematics at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, with a focus on algebraic geometry, number theory, and arithmetic geometry.

Interests

My main interests are algebraic number theory, arithmetic geometry, algebraic geometry, and commutative algebra. I am particularly interested in arithmetic questions studied through algebraic and geometric structures.

Outside mathematics

I spend much of my free time bouldering and playing chess. I enjoy training for climbing competitions and have played competitive chess for many years.
